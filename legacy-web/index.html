Everything you need

One app for the whole money picture

From a quick coffee log to your gold, shares and card bills — Pocket handles it without spreadsheets.

AI receipt scanning

Snap a photo and Gemini reads the line items, maps them to your categories, and pre-fills a split transaction. Just review and save.

Multi-currency, live rates

100+ currencies with rates refreshed automatically. Per-account currencies and cross-currency transfers, all converted to your home currency.

Smart budgets

Budget a category, several sub-categories, or a whole group. Gregorian or Hijri months, optional rollover, and a per-category breakdown.

Hijri calendar & miqaats

See Hijri dates alongside Gregorian throughout the app, with miqaats from the Mumineen calendar and Hijri-month budgeting.

Reports & insights

Net-worth over time, spending by category, daily trends and your biggest transactions — clear charts, no setup.

Accounts & transfers

Bank, cash, card, savings & investments — grouped and in the order you like, with splits, tags and one-tap transfers. Get a nudge when a balance runs low, and keep a business account out of your totals.

Everything you owe, in one place

Loans, card bills, bills due and delivery dues add up to one “You owe” total, each with a Pay button. Bank or car loans can be paid back in monthly instalments — see how many are paid and what’s next.

Regulars: bills, deliveries & shortcuts

A calendar of everything that repeats. Monthly bills (or ones that ask for the amount, like electricity), milk and newspaper deliveries with what you owe each vendor, and one-tap shortcuts for things you buy often.

Family sharing & sync

Share chosen accounts with family — view-only or with edit access — and stay in sync across all your devices in real time.

Import & export

Migrate from any app with a CSV — accounts, categories, transfers and splits auto-created. Export to JSON or CSV anytime.

Gold, silver & shares

Track grams and purity, or how many shares. See what you paid, what it’s worth today and your gain — with an alert when it’s up 20%. More

Credit cards

Set the limit, statement day and due day. See this bill, what’s left to spend, and pay it with one tap — with a reminder before it’s due.

Tidy categories

Rename, or tick a few and merge them into one — keep one of them, pick another, or make a new one. Entries, budgets and regulars move with them, and you can undo.

Help where you need it

A short guide on every main page — tap the ? by the title. And a Feedback button to tell us what to fix or build next; every message is read.

New · Pocket on Telegram

Log it the moment you spend it — just send a text

No app to open, no form to fill. Type what you spent into a Telegram chat and it lands in your Pocket book instantly — on the right account, in the right category, with today's date.

  • Plain words. 70 auto to station @taxi is an expense; put + in front for income.
  • A whole shopping trip in one message. One line per item, plus one shared line for the account or category.
  • Transfers too, even across currencies: 5000 #bank > #wallet
  • Forgot the category? The bot shows yours as buttons — one tap and it's saved.
  • Works for the family. Log into accounts shared with you, and every entry shows who added it.
  • Typo? Fix it right there. Every saved entry has ✏️ Edit and 🗑 Delete buttons.

Only you can link your chat: the code goes to your inbox, and the bot only ever sees your own accounts and the ones shared with you.

Privacy-first

Your finances are nobody else's business

Pocket stores your data in your own browser and, if you choose a plan with sync, on Pocket's cloud server too. Other users can't see it, and we look at it only if you ask us to help. No ads, no data selling, no tracking. When you scan a receipt or speak an entry, the image or recording passes through Pocket's server to Google's AI only to read it, and isn't stored.
