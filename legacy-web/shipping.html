Shipping Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version
  • Pocket sells digital services only. Nothing is posted or couriered, so there are no shipping charges.
  • What you buy is added to your Pocket account online, usually within a few minutes of payment.

1. What is delivered, and how

  • Pocket Pro, Pocket Pro Group, the introductory offer, and Pocket Plus: switched on for the account you paid from as soon as the payment is confirmed. You see the plan in the app under Settings → Account.
  • AI packs: the credits are added to your account as soon as the payment is confirmed.
  • Habit programme: a service delivered by phone and messaging over six weeks, starting on the batch's start date shown when your place is offered.

No physical goods are sold, so no delivery address is needed and nothing is shipped inside or outside India.

2. How long it takes

Plans and credits normally appear within a few minutes of a successful payment. Occasionally a payment takes longer to be confirmed by the bank or Razorpay; in that case it is applied automatically once confirmed, usually within a few hours.

3. If it hasn't arrived

If you paid and your plan or credits haven't appeared within 24 hours, write to us through the contact page with the email you sign in with, the date and amount, and the Razorpay payment ID (it starts with pay_). We will apply it or refund it, as set out in our Cancellation and Refunds policy.
