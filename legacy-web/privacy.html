Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version
  • On the Free plan, your data stays in your browser, on your device.
  • On Sync and Family, it is also stored on Pocket's cloud server, so it syncs across your devices and with the family members you choose.
  • Other users can't see your data. The people who run Pocket technically can; we look at it only when you ask us to help, or when the law requires it.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across the web.
  • A scanned receipt or voice entry goes to Google's AI only to be read, and isn't stored.
  • You can export or delete everything at any time.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Pocket ("Pocket", "the app", "we") processes, how, why, and who can see it. It covers the website, the app, the Telegram bot and our paid plans. By using Pocket you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use Pocket.

1. What data Pocket processes

  • Financial data you enter: accounts, balances, transactions, transfers, categories, budgets, debts, recurring and regular items, tags, notes and payees.
  • Account information: if you sign in, your email address, your name and a user identifier from Google sign-in.
  • Family members: the names and email addresses of people you share accounts with, and what each may do.
  • Preferences: currency, date format, theme, calendar and similar settings.
  • Plan, payment and usage records: your plan and its dates; for each payment, what was bought, the amount and Razorpay's order and payment references; and a record of each AI scan or voice entry (when, by whom, for which book, and the credit used).
  • Telegram: if you link the bot, your Telegram chat ID and the email it is linked to; and, if you turn on group summaries, the group's ID, name and settings.

2. Where your data is stored

  • On your device: Pocket stores your data in your browser's storage. On the Free plan it stays there and is not sent to us.
  • On Pocket's cloud server: on the Sync and Family plans, your data is also stored in Pocket's database, hosted by Supabase, so it is available on your devices and to the family members you share with.

3. Who can see your data

  • You.
  • Family members you share with see only the accounts you share with them, at the access level you choose. You can remove them at any time.
  • Members of a Telegram group, if the owner of a shared space turns on group summaries there: they see that space's totals, spending by category and who logged how many entries. They never see personal accounts or individual entries.
  • Other Pocket users cannot see your data. The database itself enforces this for every request from the app.
  • The people who run Pocket can technically access data stored on the cloud server, as the operators of the service; your data is not end-to-end encrypted. We look at it only to fix a problem you have asked us to help with, to keep the service secure (for example, to investigate abuse), or when the law requires us to. We do not browse it, analyse it for marketing, or share it. Data that stays on your device (the Free plan) is not visible to us.
  • Service providers who run parts of Pocket for us, only as needed for their part: Supabase (hosting and sign-in), Google (sign-in, and reading scanned receipts and voice entries), Telegram (delivering bot messages) and Razorpay (payments). Each handles data under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Sign-in

Signing in is optional; you need it for cloud sync, the Telegram bot, family sharing and AI scanning. Sign-in uses Google through Supabase Auth. We receive your email address, name and an account identifier. We never receive your Google password.

5. Telegram bot and group summaries

  • When you send an entry to the Pocket bot, Telegram delivers the message to Pocket's server, which saves it as a transaction in your book (or a book shared with you). Messages to bots are not end-to-end encrypted by Telegram.
  • While the bot waits for you to choose a category or correct an entry, it keeps that step briefly; it is removed when you finish and expires after 30 minutes.
  • In a group, the bot only reacts to commands addressed to it. It does not read the group's conversation and never records entries from a group.
  • Group summaries count only the accounts in the shared space chosen for that group. The space's owner turns them on and off, and can hide who logged entries.
  • /unlink disconnects a chat, and /summary off stops a group's summaries.

6. AI receipt scanning and voice entry

  • The receipt image or voice recording, with your category names, is sent over an encrypted connection to Pocket's server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini AI using Pocket's API key.
  • Pocket's server doesn't store the image or recording. It keeps only the usage record described above, so your AI credits can be counted.
  • Google processes the request to read amounts, merchants and items, under Google's API terms and privacy policy.

If you never scan a receipt or speak an entry, no images or recordings are sent anywhere.

7. Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. Your card, UPI and bank details go directly to Razorpay; Pocket never sees or stores them. We keep the record of what you bought, the amount and Razorpay's references, to apply your plan or credits and to help if something goes wrong.

8. Exchange rates

To convert between currencies, Pocket fetches public exchange-rate tables from open.er-api.com. These requests contain no personal or financial information.

9. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal or financial data.
  • We do not show advertising, or use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • We do not use your financial data for marketing.

10. Cookies and local storage

Pocket uses your browser's storage to keep your data and preferences on your device, and sign-in tokens to keep you signed in. These are needed for the app to work, not for advertising or tracking.

11. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Export: download all your data from Settings at any time (JSON, CSV, Excel or PDF).
  • On your device: reset all data from Settings, or clear your browser storage. Signing out clears the copy on that device.
  • When a paid plan ends: your cloud copy stays readable for 30 days so you can download it, and is then kept (without sync) so it comes back if you renew. Ask us to delete it at any time.
  • Deletion: to delete your cloud data and account, write to us through the contact page. Payment records may be kept for as long as the law requires.

12. Security

Connections to Pocket, Supabase, Google, Telegram and Razorpay use encrypted HTTPS. Supabase encrypts the stored database, and the database's access rules keep each user's data separate from other users'. As explained in section 3, the data is not end-to-end encrypted, so the people who run Pocket can technically access it; we keep that access to the few people who run the service. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

13. Children

Pocket is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so it can be removed.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

15. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data, or a request to delete it? See our contact page.
