Cancellation and Refunds

Last updated: November 2026

The short version
  • Plans, the trial and AI packs are not refundable.
  • Nothing renews automatically, so there is nothing to cancel: a plan just ends on its end date.
  • Charged twice, or money taken for a payment that failed? You get it back in full.

1. Plans and the trial

Payments for Pocket Pro (monthly or yearly) and for the ₹149 introductory offer are final and not refundable, including for the part of a period you don't use. Before you pay, the app shows exactly what you are buying and for how long, and the Free version of Pocket lets you try everything on your device first.

Switching plans. If you move from one paid plan to another, the time left on your current plan is not refunded; it is turned into extra days on the new plan, valued at what you paid per day. Moving to a bigger plan: you pay its full price and get those extra days on top. Moving to a smaller plan: there is nothing to pay, and your time left becomes your time on the new plan. The app shows how many days you will get before you confirm. Plans given free of charge and the trial carry no time over.

2. Cancellation

Plans are one-time payments for a fixed period and never renew automatically. There is no subscription to cancel and you will not be charged again unless you choose to pay. When a plan ends, your cloud copy stays readable for 30 days so you can download your data; Pocket keeps working on your device.

3. AI packs

AI packs are not refundable. Credits are valid for one year from the date of purchase; credits left in a pack after that expire. If a scan or voice entry fails because of the AI service, its credits are returned to you automatically. Credits used on a recording or picture that was unclear are not returned, so speak clearly and photograph the whole receipt.

Pocket Plus (scheduled Google Drive backups, ₹199 once) is not refundable. You can try backing up and restoring for free before you buy.

4. Payment errors (always refunded)

  • Charged twice for the same purchase, or charged by mistake: the extra payment is refunded in full.
  • Money taken but the payment failed (your plan or credits didn't arrive): it is usually reversed automatically by your bank or Razorpay within 5–7 working days. If it isn't, or if your plan didn't update after a successful payment, contact us and we will apply it or refund it.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, normally within 5–7 working days of approval (your bank may take longer to show it).

5. Habit programme

The habit programme fee is not refundable once your place in a batch is confirmed. If you are on the waitlist, you are not charged until a place is offered to you and you accept it.

6. How to raise a payment problem

Write to us through the contact page within 30 days of the payment, with the email you sign in with, the date and amount, and the Razorpay payment ID (it starts with pay_ and is in Razorpay's confirmation email). We reply within 2 working days.
